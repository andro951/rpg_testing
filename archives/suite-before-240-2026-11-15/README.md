Original templates, generated definitions, preview prompts, current result/coverage/optimization evidence, and relevant catalog/config snapshots are preserved byte-for-byte. The archived result directories were cleared from their active locations only after verifying these copies. Historical evidence is not rescored or relabeled. This snapshot is not an active benchmark suite and does not authorize inference. No model inference occurred during archival.
