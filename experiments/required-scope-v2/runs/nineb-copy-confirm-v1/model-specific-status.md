These results are conditional on the tested tasks and generated recipes. They do not show that a method is ready for all production tasks. Safe generated IDs make reserved-character selector errors less applicable, while exact text, unrelated state and nested identity targeting still matter. The VL8, 4B and 9B confirmation cases are fresh, but their candidates were selected using previous holdouts; they are follow-ups, not untouched model-selection evidence. The VL8 gains were six on reserved-ID nested update, six on reserved-ID removal and three on numeric update. Both arms passed all nine observations on each remaining fixture. For 4B, literal v2 gained nine on literal backslashes and six on quotes but lost nine on boundary spaces. On the 9B confirmation, each 9B model had identical original/copy-v1 outcomes for every matched observation. Neither the prior outputs nor these confirmations should be used to revise the candidates. Any future prompt changes require separate development cases and fresh evaluation.
