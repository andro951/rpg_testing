How to write a path:
A path is a bracketed list of unquoted steps, separated by commas. Start at the whole database. At an object, a step selects an existing field; at an array, a step selects the element by its unique ID or primitive value, never by position. Use as many steps as needed. Each selector must match exactly one element; reject ambiguous typed identities.
In each step, letters, digits, hyphens and underscores are literal. Encode every other UTF-8 byte as %HH: comma is %2C, brackets %5B and %5D, dot %2E, percent %25, space %20, quote %22, slash %2F and colon %3A. An empty step is %_. Commas between steps are separators, not part of a step. Example: [items, item-83, name].
